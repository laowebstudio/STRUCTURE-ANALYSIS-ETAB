SAPUDOM Structure Analysis V1.47.2.1
Easy Slab Input + Validation Fix

Changes
- Slab DL and LL are entered as positive downward magnitudes.
- Solver applies slab gravity in Global -Z automatically.
- Backward-compatible with old saved negative slab loads by using load magnitude internally.
- Live slab-load preview shows self-weight, total DL, LL, and direction.
- Slab thickness validation: 80-500 mm.
- Concrete E validation: 10,000-60,000 MPa.
- Poisson ratio validation: 0 <= nu < 0.5.
- Concrete unit weight and non-negative DL/LL validation.
- Story-height list automatically expands/trims to match Number of stories.
- Slab shell/plate FEM from V1.47.2 remains unchanged except gravity sign handling.

Example
150 mm slab, gamma=24 kN/m3, superimposed DL=4.5 kN/m2, LL=2.0 kN/m2:
Self-weight = 3.60 kN/m2
Total DL = 8.10 kN/m2 downward
LL = 2.00 kN/m2 downward
