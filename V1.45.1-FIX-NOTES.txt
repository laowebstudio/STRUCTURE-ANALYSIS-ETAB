SAPUDOM Structure Analysis V1.45.1
Advanced Loads → RC Design Synchronization Fix

BUG FIXED
3D Moment/Shear diagrams responded to newly assigned Advanced Member Loads, but RC Beam Design / 3D Rebar Viewer could continue reading a cached pre-load-change V1.40 envelope. This left Support-i / Midspan / Support-j Vu and reinforcement zoning stale.

FIX
1. Advanced load assignment / clear invalidates derived envelope and RC result caches.
2. Analyze 3D invalidates any older envelope after the new solve.
3. RC Beam Design always rebuilds the governing load-combination envelope on open/recalculate.
4. RC input settings and member overrides are preserved.

TEST
Use M28 with a Point Load near j, Analyze 3D, then open RC Beam Design and 3D Rebar Viewer. Vu and reinforcement zones must change when the solver/load-combination demand changes.
