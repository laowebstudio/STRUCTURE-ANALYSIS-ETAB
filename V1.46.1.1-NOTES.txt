SAPUDOM Structure Analysis V1.46.1.1
RC Beam Station-Based Auto Design + Economical Reinforcement Zoning

BASE
- Developed directly from V1.46.1 Whole Model Solve / Selected Member Display Fix.
- Whole Model solve semantics remain protected. Member selection remains display-only.

NEW RC BEAM DESIGN PIPELINE
1. Re-solves the current complete 3D model for active load combinations, load cases and active pattern.
2. Recovers member actions at configurable stations along each beam (default 41, range 21-101).
3. Builds station envelopes for P/N, V2, V3, T, M2 and M3 with governing analysis labels.
4. RC beam demand is taken from the station envelope, not only member end forces.
5. Positive station bending demand drives bottom longitudinal reinforcement zoning.
6. Negative station bending demand drives top longitudinal reinforcement zoning.
7. Shear station envelope drives variable stirrup spacing zones; the old assumed Midspan Vu = 25% end shear is removed from auto zoning.
8. Every shear station is checked against the stirrup zone actually provided at that station.
9. Top and bottom flexural capacities are checked independently.
10. Extra longitudinal-bar cut-off zones are extended by calculated development length.
11. Economical zoning can be toggled. It retains minimum continuous bars and adds extra bars only where station demand requires them.
12. RC Design Details reports station count, governing station, bottom/top zones, stirrup zones and an indicative longitudinal steel reduction versus uniform maximum reinforcement.

IMPORTANT ENGINEERING SCOPE
- Torsion design is not yet included in RC beam reinforcement design even though T is recovered in the station envelope.
- Seismic special moment-frame detailing, serviceability/crack control, deflection design, splice staggering and final BBS optimization remain outside this version.
- Estimated steel saving is a zoning comparison before final anchorage/lap/BBS quantities and must not be treated as a final project cost quantity.
- Final construction design still requires project-specific code verification by a qualified engineer.

T7 Top zoning: negative support moment should increase top bars in the support region.

T9 Development: extra-bar zones should extend beyond their demand cut-off by calculated ld.
T10 Station shear verification: each station should PASS only when the local provided stirrup zone capacity/spacing passes.
