SAPUDOM Structure Analysis V1.47.2 — Slab Shell/Plate Analysis Engine

Scope
- Adds solver-linked 4-node Q4 shell/plate elements for horizontal floor slabs.
- Membrane formulation: plane-stress Q4.
- Plate formulation: Mindlin-Reissner bending + transverse shear with reduced shear integration.
- Slab DOFs are assembled into the same global 6-DOF frame nodes: Ux, Uy, Uz, Rx, Ry.
- Gravity area loads are applied directly to slab finite elements, not converted to perimeter-beam tributary UDLs.
- Slab self-weight can be included automatically in DL.
- Reports center-point w, M11, M22, M12, V13 and V23 for each slab panel.
- Load Cases and linear Load Combinations carry slab results through the existing whole-model solve path.

Easy input
- Generate + analyze floor slabs checkbox
- Thickness
- Concrete E
- Poisson ratio
- Superimposed DL
- LL
- Concrete unit weight + self-weight checkbox
- Mesh is automatically compatible 1x1 per structural bay in this release.

Engineering limitation
V1.47.2 is a first solver-integrated slab FEM foundation. The current compatible mesh is one Q4 element per bay and the reported shell actions are element-center values. It is not yet a production-grade refined mesh/design-strip engine, and RC slab reinforcement design remains frozen/out of scope. Benchmark verification is required before construction design use.
