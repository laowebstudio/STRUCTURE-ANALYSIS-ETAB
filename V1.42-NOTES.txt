SAPUDOM Structure Analysis V1.42 — 3D RC Rebar Visualization Foundation

Added:
- Calculation-linked 3D RC Beam Rebar Viewer available from RC Beam Design Details and RC Beam Construction Drawing.
- Visualizes bottom longitudinal bars using the verified bar count and automatic layer arrangement.
- Visualizes stirrups at the calculated/provided spacing.
- Visualizes automatic 90° anchorage hooks at i/j ends when selected by Development/Anchorage verification.
- Semi-transparent concrete beam envelope with member i/j labels.
- Interactive Rotate, Zoom, Fit and Isometric controls.
- Visibility toggles for Concrete, Stirrups and Main Bars.
- Viewer is read-only and does not modify RC design quantities.

Protected:
- V1.41.6.2 Manual Main Rebar and Auto/Manual switching.
- V1.41.6.1 vertical-fit safety and scaled 2D rebar drawing.
- Development / anchorage / lap splice logic including automatic Class A/B verification.
- Existing 2D and 3D analysis engines.

Phase 1 limitation:
- Top reinforcement, column cages, beam-column joints, slab reinforcement, splice staggering and full-building rebar federation are not yet generated.
