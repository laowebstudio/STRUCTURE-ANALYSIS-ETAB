SAPUDOM Structure Analysis V1.42.1
3D RC Rebar View Controls

Changes from V1.42:
- Added deterministic camera presets to the RC Beam 3D Rebar Viewer:
  Isometric, Side, Top, End-i and End-j.
- End-i / End-j allow direct cross-section inspection of multi-layer longitudinal bars.
- Fit remains available after manual rotation/zoom.
- Existing V1.42 rebar geometry and V1.41.6.2 RC design logic are unchanged.
- Viewer remains read-only.

T1 target:
For M28 400x600, 9Ø20 with L1=6Ø20 + L2=3Ø20, press End-i or End-j and verify 6+3 bars.
