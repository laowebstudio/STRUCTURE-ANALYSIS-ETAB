SAPUDOM V1.41.1 — RC Beam Shear Design Verification

BASE
V1.41 RC Beam Design from 3D Governing Envelope.

NEW SHEAR VERIFICATION
- Uses governing Vu from 3D Envelope.
- Calculates concrete shear contribution Vc.
- Calculates provided stirrup shear contribution Vs.
- Calculates nominal shear strength Vn = Vc + Vs.
- Calculates design shear strength phi*Vn.
- Calculates Demand/Capacity Ratio: Vu / (phi*Vn).
- Shows PASS when Vu <= phi*Vn.
- Calculates required Av/s and compares with provided Av/s.
- Checks stirrup spacing against a practical ACI-style maximum spacing limit.
- Adds simplified maximum shear ceiling check.
- Table now shows phiVn, Vu/phiVn and Shear Status.
- Beam Details shows:
  Vu
  phiVc
  Vs provided
  phiVn
  Vu/phiVn
  stirrup spacing
  maximum spacing
  PASS/FAIL for shear strength, spacing and max shear limit.

IMPORTANT
This remains engineering design-assist software, not final code approval.
The V1.41.1 shear layer improves traceability but does not yet replace a complete
code-specific design check, seismic detailing review, anchorage/development checks,
torsion design, or engineer-of-record verification.
