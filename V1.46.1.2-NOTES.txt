SAPUDOM Structure Analysis V1.46.1.2
Practical RC Beam Detailing + Constructability Optimization

Base: V1.46.1.1

Changes:
- Keeps Whole Model -> Station Envelope RC design pipeline.
- Automatic longitudinal reinforcement is arranged in symmetric bar pairs (2,4,6,8...).
- Minimum 2 continuous top and bottom bars are retained.
- Theoretical cut-off zones are extended by calculated development length.
- Very short extra-bar zones are expanded to a practical minimum length.
- Detailing cut-off points are snapped to 50 mm increments.
- Overlapping zones are rebuilt as a clean non-overlapping practical envelope.
- 3D Rebar Viewer now draws station detailing zones instead of drawing the governing maximum bottom cage over the full beam length.
- Top 3D bars also follow station detailing zones.

IMPORTANT: design-assist only until benchmarked and reviewed by a licensed structural engineer for the applicable project/code.
