SAPUDOM Structure Analysis V1.44.1 — Demand-Linked Stirrup Zoning Fix

Fix target
- V1.44 correctly split Support-i / Midspan / Support-j, but all three zones could still show the same spacing because the general d/2 maximum spacing controlled every zone.

What changed
- Auto stirrup zoning now keeps the local end-shear calculation for each support zone.
- When local factored end shear exceeds phi*Vc, the support zone receives a conservative detailing-assist spacing cap of min(d/4, 150 mm), rounded down to the 25 mm spacing grid.
- Midspan keeps the normal local demand/code spacing, allowing wider spacing when shear demand falls.
- 3D viewer reports each zone's Vu and shows the support detailing cap when active.
- Manual stirrup mode remains uniform and unchanged.

Verification boundary
- The support densification cap is a conservative detailing-assist rule, not a claim that d/4 is universally code-mandated.
- Full station-by-station shear envelope and project-specific seismic detailing remain future verification work.
