SAPUDOM Structure Analysis V1.42.2
True Section Cut Rebar Rendering

Changes
- End-i and End-j now render a true midspan section cut instead of looking at the anchorage hook geometry.
- Longitudinal reinforcement is shown as circular bar cross-sections at the calculation-linked layer coordinates.
- Multi-layer arrangements such as 9Ø20 = L1 6Ø20 + L2 3Ø20 are directly countable in End views.
- Stirrup and concrete section are drawn in the same true section coordinate system.
- End view title explicitly identifies TRUE SECTION CUT @ MIDSPAN.
- Dragging from an End view returns to the normal rotatable 3D view.
- RC design, anchorage, lap splice, manual-main-bar and verification logic are unchanged from V1.41.6.2 / V1.42.1.
