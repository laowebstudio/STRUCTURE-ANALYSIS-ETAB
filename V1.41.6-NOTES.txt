SAPUDOM Structure Analysis V1.41.6
Automatic Anchorage Solution + Rebar Arrangement Verification

Built from V1.41.5.1.

Added
- Automatic multi-layer bottom reinforcement arrangement.
- Horizontal clear-spacing verification per layer.
- Vertical layer-fit verification.
- Reinforcement centroid calculation and centroid-aware effective depth d_eff.
- Flexural capacity recheck after multi-layer centroid shift; automatic bar-count increase when required.
- Automatic anchorage solution: Straight development first, then standard 90-degree hook design-assist when straight ld is unavailable.
- Hook development length ldh and 90-degree hook tail shown in calculation data.
- RC Beam Drawing shows selected anchorage method at i/j and draws hook geometry when automatically selected.
- Multi-layer arrangements can now PASS when fit/spacing/cover/vertical geometry are verified instead of always forcing MULTI-LAYER REVIEW.

Protected / unchanged
- Existing 2D analysis engine.
- 3D stiffness-analysis path.
- Load combinations/envelope logic.
- V1.41.5.1 automatic Class A/B lap-splice eligibility logic.

Important engineering limitation
- Automatic 90-degree hook is a design-assist check. Project-specific support dimensions, side cover, confinement, seismic requirements, top-bar design, torsion, serviceability and splice location/staggering still require engineering review before construction issue.
