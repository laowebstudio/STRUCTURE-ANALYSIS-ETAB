SAPUDOM Structure Analysis V1.47.2.3
Slab FEM Bending Moment + Shear Force Recovery Fix

Purpose
- Fix the T2 condition where a symmetric 1-bay slab could deflect while M11/M22/M12 and V13/V23 recovered as zero.

Changes
- Replaced one-Q4-per-bay bending recovery with a solver-internal 2x2 Q4 Mindlin patch.
- Internal slab bending DOFs are statically condensed to the existing four structural corner joints, preserving Beam/Column connectivity and the whole-building global matrix interface.
- Added selective transverse-shear integration with small stabilization to suppress hourglass modes while limiting shear locking.
- After the global solve, internal slab DOFs are back-substituted and plate moments/shears are recovered from sub-element fields.
- Slab FEM reports signed envelope M11, M22, M12, V13 and V23 and center deflection w.
- V1.47.2.2 full-regeneration/reset integrity remains active.
