SAPUDOM Structure Analysis V1.44 — Beam Reinforcement Zoning

Scope
- Top negative-moment support zones: Support-i / Midspan / Support-j.
- Variable stirrup zones: Support-i / Midspan / Support-j.
- Top support quantity in Auto mode is calculation-linked to negative end-moment envelope.
- Stirrup spacing in Auto mode is calculation-linked to end shear envelope; midspan uses a reduced interpolated demand.
- 3D viewer and beam drawing display the zoning.

Important verification boundary
- Support-zone lengths are detailing-assist, using a preliminary support-zone rule.
- Full station-by-station moment/shear envelope, top anchorage/development, seismic detailing, torsion, serviceability, and splice staggering are not yet code-verified.
